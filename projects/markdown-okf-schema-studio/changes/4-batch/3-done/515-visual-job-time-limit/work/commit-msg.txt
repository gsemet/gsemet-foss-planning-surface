ci: split the visual checks into two jobs that run in parallel

The screenshot campaign no longer runs out of its 15-minute CI limit. It
runs as two jobs side by side, visual and visual-2, and the report job
merges their results. Every scenario and check still runs, the limit is
unchanged and no retry is added.

Locally, "just ci-visual" still runs the whole campaign, and
"just ci-visual 1" or "just ci-visual 2" runs one part.
