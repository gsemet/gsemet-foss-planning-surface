## DIAG-084 assertion change (constitution section 3)

- The old test `the JAR pipeline starts one process per diagram before any of them finishes`
  asserted that 3 processes exist before the first answers. That contradicts the SwRS limit of 2
  processes at once. It passed only by waiting for the 30 s process timeout (30016 ms measured).
- The new test `the JAR pipeline runs one process per diagram, at most 2 at once, results in
  document order` asserts what the SwRS states:
  - 2 processes start, and the third waits;
  - the third starts when one process finishes;
  - 3 processes in all, with a peak of 2;
  - the pictures come back in document order, although the processes finish in another order (b, c, a);
  - a document without PlantUML starts no process.
- Time: 3.68 ms (was 30016 ms). It also asserts that the run takes under 1 s.
- The SwRS DIAG-084 known issue about the old test is removed, and its Method line is replaced.
