## What changed

- **A. Pure module.** `src/page/modules/listItemMove.ts`: `moveListItem(source, itemCount, from, to)`.
  It handles line endings, the container prefix, items and gaps, refusals, the count check, the
  indexes, ordered numbers, the content column and task items. `quotePrefixOf` is now exported from
  `src/page/modules/tableInContainer.ts`.
- **B. Page feature.** `src/page/features/listItemMove.ts`: `installListItemMove(rt)`.
  - It follows `tableRowMove.ts`. The handles sit in a layer on body. `position()` reads every box
    before it writes, in a frame. It uses a ResizeObserver and the resize event, and pushes a block
    with no room on its left.
  - A drag works on the item and on the handle strip, with drop lines.
  - Alt+Up and Alt+Down work on a handle and stop the event, so the block move never sees them.
  - A move posts `previewEditOpen`, then one final `previewEditSave`, then `postEditClosed`. It has
    a 5 s timeout, a settle timer and the focus after the render.
  - The list range is the new `listRange` in `mappedBlocks.ts`, which wraps CR-424's
    `sourceRangeForList(list, list)`.
  - Wiring:
    - `pageRuntime.ts`: the `ListItemMove` interface and member;
    - `runtime.ts`: install, and reposition;
    - `editMode.ts`: refresh, and the hover over a handle outlines its item;
    - `blockSourceEditor.ts`: `receiveSource`;
    - `pageInput.ts`: a click on a handle opens no editor.
- **C. Styles.** `LIST_ITEM_HANDLES_CSS` in `src/core/preview/styles/editModeStyles.ts`, joined into
  `EDIT_MODE_CSS`. Every rule is scoped to edit mode. It holds the layer, the handle, the push
  (no max-content), the dimmed dragged item and the before/after drop lines in
  `--studio-focusBorder`.
- **D. Requirements.**
  - New: SwRS-MDOKFSTUDIO-BLOCK-262, created with `okfreq new` as proposed. It derives from
    StRS-MDOKFSTUDIO-BLOCK-014, and its scenarios mirror SwRS-MDOKFSTUDIO-TABLE-002.
  - Changed: StRS-MDOKFSTUDIO-BLOCK-014 (proposed) gains intent, a constraint and "Path 3: Move a
    list item".
  - The generated `index.md` and `tested_in_files` of SwRS-MDOKFSTUDIO-BLOCK-130 come from
    `just requirements-update`.
  - `requirements/tiers/log.md` has a `### List item handles` entry under 2026-11-20.
- **E. Docs.**
  - `docs/user/editing.md`: a paragraph after the list item editing paragraph;
  - `.agents/glossary/common.md`: a "List item handle" entry;
  - `CHANGELOG.md`: a line under Unreleased.
