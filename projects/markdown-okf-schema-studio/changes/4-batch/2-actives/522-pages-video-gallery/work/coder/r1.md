## Finding: a click on the picture does not play (Chromium)

The spec asks for `preload="none"`, no script, and native controls. Verified in headless Chromium
156 and headless Chrome 158, and on the built page: a click in the middle of the picture does
nothing and requests nothing; the play button of the controls (bottom left), or the space bar on the
focused video, starts it. With `preload="metadata"` a picture click plays, but bytes load before the
click. Not verified in Firefox or Safari, nor in a headed window.

Evidence: `evidence/click-probe.txt`, `evidence/videos-page-checks.txt` (`card 0`, `card 1`).

Owner answer `vidéos-Q3` reads "the video starts on a click". The spec is implemented as written;
whether the play button satisfies it is the spec owner's call. A picture click would need a few
lines of script (click on a not yet loaded video calls `play()`), against the spec's "no script".

Because of it, wording that would have been false was written as follows:

- page intro: "Press play on a picture to watch it.";
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, feature clip guideline: "a click of its play button";
- CORE-098 S5 and its criteria name the play button, and a Known issue states the limit;
- the browser test clicks the play button by position (the controls are in a closed shadow tree), so
  a Chromium upgrade that moves the button would fail it.
