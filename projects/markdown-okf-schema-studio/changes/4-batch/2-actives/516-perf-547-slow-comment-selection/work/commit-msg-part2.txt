fix(render): save a comment without restyling a whole large document

Saving a review comment in a large document no longer restyles the whole
page, and the layout around aligned images, figures and tables stays the
same, also in an exported file. The Comment button, the copy button and the
edit mode outline now follow the selection or the pointer at most once per
frame, and they appear and disappear in the same situations as before.
