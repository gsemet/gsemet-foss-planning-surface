## What changed

B1, the Comment button after the page removed or hid the selected text:

- New `src/page/modules/shownContent.ts` (`watchShownContent`): one MutationObserver on the content
  root (child list, text, ten attributes that show, hide or relocate content, `class` and `style`
  among them) and on eight page root attributes. It looks at no record.
- `src/page/modules/commentCompose.ts`: while a selection is followed, a page change has the
  selection read again once, at the next frame. A scroll never reads it: it applies that result
  (button gone, or moved with the text). A scroll that comes before the frame is answered by the
  frame, before the paint. A selection change (`refresh`) drops the pending state. Nothing runs while
  the comment box is open or when no selection is followed. As at head, the change alone moves or
  removes nothing; the next scroll does.
- `src/page/features/scrollCore.ts`: its own observer is replaced by `watchShownContent`. This also
  fixes a round 2 difference found here: content hidden by a `style` or a `class` under an unmoved
  selection left the old selected text in the scroll report.

Cost: nothing on a scroll, nothing without a followed selection. After a page change, one
`readSelection` in that frame: it follows the selected text, the two end blocks and, for a selection
ending at offset 0, what lies between them, not the document (head did the same read at every
scroll). A selection of the whole document therefore costs its text, once per changed frame. Content
that changes at every frame while a selection is followed would be read at every such frame. The
browser also queues one record per mutation for the observer, as for any observer.

Observed: no content change made by the page itself while scrolling a generated document with
headings, wide tables, code, tab sets, notes and comments, in read and edit mode, 800 and 1200 wide
(about 190 scrolls each): 0 changes seen, 0 reads of the selection (temporary probe, removed).

Not observed by design: a change made only by a style sheet, or outside the content root and the
eight root attributes. I found no such path that shows or hides commentable text; the media queries
I saw in the preview styles act on parts that take no comment or do not hide body text.
