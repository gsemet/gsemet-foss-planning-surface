feat(editor): edit the pointed block inside admonitions and alerts

In edit mode, a click or Enter on a paragraph, list item, diagram, formula or
code block of an admonition or GitHub alert now opens that element alone. The
whole box opens from its title or frame, or when it is selected as a whole.
Move chords reorder the blocks of the box without leaving it, the caret stays
in the editor at a list edge, and an erased line of an alert keeps the alert
whole.

Checks that expected the whole box to open from a click or Enter on its body
now expect the pointed element, or open the box from its title: the owner
reversed that behaviour on 2026-11-26. The lazy alert line replay opens the
alert from its title, so what it checks stays the same.
