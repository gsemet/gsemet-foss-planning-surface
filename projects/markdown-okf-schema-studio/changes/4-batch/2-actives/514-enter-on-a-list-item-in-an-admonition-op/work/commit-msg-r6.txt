fix(editor): keep formulas and HTML tags whole in the blocks of a box

In an admonition or an alert, a click or Enter no longer opens only part of a
formula, or lines that take the closing tag of an HTML element around them:
where the lines of an element cannot be told, the block or box around it opens.
The title of a callout written as a div opens the whole callout. The move chord
also refuses to start a callout with a heading or a `---` line when its class is
given by the options of a div.
