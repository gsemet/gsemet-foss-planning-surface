fix(editor): copy, cut and paste work on a list followed by blank lines

Writers can copy, cut and paste after a list that has blank lines
after it in edit mode, instead of meeting "The preview is out of date".
A copy or cut leaves out the blank lines after the list, a cut keeps
one blank line between the blocks around it, and a paste after the list
gets one blank line on each side.
