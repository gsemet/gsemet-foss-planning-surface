# Feature clip: a very wide table in Auto width

This clip shows what the Width button's Auto mode does for a very wide table. The page opens in
Margin, the default: the text sits in a centred column and the table of deploys per month is cut at
that column, so most of the year is hidden and the table scrolls sideways. One click on the Width
button (row 3 of the floating toolbar) sets Auto: the table breaks out of the column, takes the
width it needs, centred, and the whole year shows at once, while the text stays in its narrow
column. A reader keeps a comfortable line length and still sees a wide table whole.

It is made with the merged clip kit (`docs/demo-video/clip-kit/`) and the feature clip recipe
(`docs/demo-video/guidelines/feature-clip-process.guidelines.md`): two captions in turn, orange
marks in turn, 1280x720 at 15 fps, no card, the logo top right, a loop, about 10.6 s (12 s at most
in the fallback of the loop). The clip lives in `docs/demo-video/clips/wide-table-auto-width/`.

The storyboard draft is `work/storyboard/clip.md`, with the stage in `work/storyboard/stage/`. The
batch films the trial take from the draft, puts its pictures in the storyboard, renders the clip
and opens the PR with the clip watchable (the GIF inline, the MP4 and the WebM linked at the PR's
head). The owner reviews the storyboard and the clip in that draft PR (the Q3 rule).
