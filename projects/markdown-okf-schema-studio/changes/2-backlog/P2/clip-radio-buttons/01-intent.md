# Feature clip: pick an option, the file follows

This clip shows radio choice lists. A writer types list items that start with `( )` or `(x)`, and
Studio shows them as one radio group, labelled by the paragraph above. A click on another option
selects it at once and moves the `x` in the Markdown file: two characters on two lines, one undo
step. A plan can ask its reader to decide, and the reader decides with one click instead of
editing the text by hand.

The clip films Studio in reading mode on the left and the same file as raw text on the right, so
the viewer sees the syntax, the rendered group and the file changing in the same shot. It is one
continuous shot of about 10.5 s: the click, then a zoom on the raw text with two marks, the new
`(x)` and the old option now `( )`.

It is made with the merged clip kit (`docs/demo-video/clip-kit/`) and the feature clip recipe
(`docs/demo-video/guidelines/feature-clip-process.guidelines.md`): two captions in turn, orange
marks in turn, 1280x720 at 15 fps, no card, the logo top right, a loop. The clip lives in
`docs/demo-video/clips/radio-buttons/`.

The storyboard draft is `work/storyboard/clip.md`, with the stage in `work/storyboard/stage/`. The
batch films the trial take from this draft, puts its pictures in the storyboard, renders the clip
and opens the PR as a draft with the clip watchable (the GIF inline, the MP4 and the WebM linked at
the PR's head). The owner reviews the storyboard and the clip in that PR (radio-Q3).
