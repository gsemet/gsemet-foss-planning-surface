## 460 px concern (checked first, reproduced)

Reproduced by running the scenario through capture.py with the manifest width forced to 460 and 520
(scratch script outside the repo): 460 fails with "sticky heading overlay is hidden after scrolling",
520 passes.

Cause. The scenario asserts that a three-heading ancestry band grows beyond 3 lines and is visible
after the harness positions h3[data-line=5] just under the band (capture.py:508 `stickyHeading`).
The sticky band reserves `--sphinx-preview-toolbar-width` (toolbar width + 22 px, scrollSync.ts:474,
previewStyles.ts:468). The toolbar is as wide as its widest row; row 2 now has 5 buttons (166 px)
instead of row 1's 4, so the heading column loses about 30 px. Measured with a probe at the
document's maximum scroll: band height 363 px at 460 vs 261 px at 520. The fixture
(10-sticky-heading-wrap, 4 short blocks) can only scroll 47 px at 460, which is less than the band
height, so the harness's `scrollBy(h3.top - band.bottom + 4)` is negative, clamps to scrollY 0, and
the band hides (top of file). So the failure is a fixture scroll-range limit with a pathological
heading, not a product defect: the band is still visible and correct whenever the document can
scroll, nothing overlaps the toolbar (that is what the reserved width is for), and the
other 460 px sticky scenario (`preview-sticky-headings-3-light-narrow`) still passes. Widening the
toolbar by one 30 px button is an accepted cost of the 9-button toolbar; no layout fix is needed.

- [Low, advisory] test/visual/scenarios.ts:1123-1126: widening to 520 drops 460 px coverage for the
  natural-growth case. Preferred (non-blocking) follow-up: keep `...NARROW` (460) and add filler
  paragraphs at the end of test/visual/fixtures/10-sticky-heading-wrap.md so the document scrolls
  further than the band height (about 400 px extra), then remove `width: 520`. The comment
  "keeps the wrap at three lines" is also inaccurate (it asserts more than 3 lines); reword it.
