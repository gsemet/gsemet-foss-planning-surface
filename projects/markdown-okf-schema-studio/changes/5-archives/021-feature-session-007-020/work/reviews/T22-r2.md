## MEDIUM fix
- Textarea `mousedown` and `input` call `edit.closeLinkPanel(false)`: panel destroyed, no refocus or selection restore, shift given back. It posts nothing, so there is no double save. The `closed` flag keeps the panel's own blur silent. The textarea blur relaxation is unchanged.

- `closeLinkPanel` is cleared on close and `closeBlockEdit` still destroys the panel.

## LOW fixes
- `[` and `]` are escaped in the text. `LINK_PATTERN` accepts `\.` escapes and `linkDraft` unescapes on read-back.
- `<` and `>` in the URL are percent-encoded. Whitespace or unbalanced parentheses still get `<...>`, which can no longer contain `>`.
- The title is kept on replace and the caret math includes it.
- Cmd/Ctrl+K inside a field is consumed and focuses the URL field. Alt/Shift variants are ignored.
