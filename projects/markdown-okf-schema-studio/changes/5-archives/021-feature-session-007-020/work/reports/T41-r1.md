## Behaviour
- With an active selection, Alt+Up/Down (target a block, a bar, body or the root) posts one `previewBlockMove` with
  line..endLine = the whole span and the neighbour beyond its end. No neighbour (document end) or an included
  neighbour: key consumed, nothing posted, selection kept. Key repeat is dropped during the flight (T37 flag).
- Bar arrows: only the bar of the selection's last block moves the span; other bars move their single block.
- Without a selection T37 behaviour is unchanged (test).
- Restoration (review focus): the new selection is stored by ordinal + block count (`anchor+-1`, `target+-1`,
  `count`) in `pendingMoveSelection`, also written to the webview saved state.
  - Patch path: the flight settles on result+render and re-selects via `selectBlocksNow` (focus on the free end).
  - Full reload: the page restarts with no flight; `resumeMoveSelection` (in `initializeContent`) applies the saved
    state once the document version differs from the move's.
  - A different block count, a different document, an open editor or edit mode off select nothing, never another
    block. A refusal or the 5 s timer clears it; the old selection stays after a refusal.
- Host: planBlockMove moves the span as one block (inner separators untouched), one WorkspaceEdit = one undo step.
