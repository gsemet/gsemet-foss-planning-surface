## Candidate StRS (use cases)

- UC-TOOLBAR-1: A reader finds the floating toolbar's actions in three rows that never overlap the text
  or each other, read-only previews hide Edit, and the reader can collapse it to its first row.
- UC-TOOLBAR-2: A reader's view choices (toolbar collapsed, table of contents, width, zoom) are remembered
  for the workspace and apply in read-only previews too.
- UC-TOOLBAR-3: While scrolling, the reader always sees which section they are in (sticky headings, with a
  configurable depth), without the band moving the text.
- UC-TOOLBAR-4: A reader shows a table of contents column beside the document, folds sections, sees the
  current section and jumps to a heading, without the column covering the content.
- UC-TOOLBAR-5: A reader follows links and anchors and comes back with Back and Next, to the exact place,
  without losing what is typed in an open editor.
- UC-TOOLBAR-6: Links in the preview open the right target (document, line, anchor, external address) or are
  refused with a reason, and never leave the workspace.
- UC-TOOLBAR-7: A reader decides per panel whether the preview and the text editor scroll together, and can
  see which block the editor cursor is in.

## Notable findings

- The brief and C-INT-007 say Back/Next are refused while a block editor is open; the code (CR 025) saves and
  closes the editor first and navigates (F-TOOLBAR-033).
- The sticky band follows h1-h6 including headings inside admonitions and properties, the TOC lists h1-h4 and
  excludes them (F-TOOLBAR-017).
- Link target hints (missing, blocked) are only a tooltip; the link looks valid (UX-41, F-TOOLBAR-041); the
  floating toolbar still overlaps content and the find widget (UX-44, F-TOOLBAR-005).
