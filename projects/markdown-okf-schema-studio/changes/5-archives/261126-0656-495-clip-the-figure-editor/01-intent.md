# Feature clip: edit a figure in one form

This clip shows the figure editor: in edit mode, the Edit figure button over a figure opens one
form with the image source, the alt text, the caption, the legend, the width, the alignment and the
name (the label other pages refer to). The writer changes the caption, sets the width to 40%,
aligns the figure right and gives it a name, then applies: the form writes a clean `{figure}`
directive, with the new option lines and the untouched ones kept as they were, and the page shows
the smaller figure floating right of the text. Nobody has to remember the option names or the
directive layout.

It is made with the merged clip kit (`docs/demo-video/clip-kit/`) and the feature clip recipe
(`docs/demo-video/guidelines/feature-clip-process.guidelines.md`): two captions in turn, orange
marks in turn, 1280x720 at 15 fps, no card, the logo top right, a loop, 12 s. The clip lives in
`docs/demo-video/clips/figure-editor/`.

The storyboard draft is `work/storyboard/clip.md`, with the stage in `work/storyboard/stage/` (a
page and the small SVG picture its figure shows). By figure-editor-Q3, the batch films the trial take
from this draft, puts its pictures in the storyboard, renders the clip and opens the PR as a draft
with the clip watchable; the owner reviews the storyboard and the clip in that PR.
