## Failure 2: check_edit_palette_tall (long-chapter dark, sticky-3)
Cause: the "box top in view" state scrolled with a constant clearance `max(220, 114 + 4 + bar_height + 24)`.
114 is the toolbar bottom on a local machine; on Linux CI the sticky headings band (sticky-3) reaches
lower (bar stuck at top 162.56) and the band height changes with the scroll position (it shows the
headings of the current position), so the box top ended up too close/far and the bar was in its
stuck state, not attached.
Fix: the clearance is now derived from the live layout: `max(220, max(band bottom, toolbar bottom) + 4
+ bar height + 24)`, and the scroll is repeated (up to 6 times, 200 ms each) until the box top sits
between that clearance and clearance + 60 px, since the band changes with the position. The assertions
that follow (attached within 6 px, not covered) are unchanged. Same fix for `check_table_toolbar_tall`
(same constant 114 there; probe on `[data-sphinx-table-menu]` and `main > table`), which would have
failed the same way in a sticky-3 configuration with a taller band.
Could not reproduce the CI font metrics locally (macOS passes before and after); the fix follows from the
CI numbers (host 139, bar stuck at 162 = band bottom) and the code.
