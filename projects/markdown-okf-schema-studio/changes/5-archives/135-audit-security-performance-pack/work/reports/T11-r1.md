- After: first 769, second 367, ratio 0.48 (range 0.40-0.52).
- The whole-render ratio is already under 0.5 before the change (JIT/grammar warm-up dominates the first render) and is noise-limited; it does not discriminate. - Open point: the "second under 50% of first" proof holds in 6 of 7 after-runs (0.51/0.52 in two); it is a warm-up effect more than a cache effect.
