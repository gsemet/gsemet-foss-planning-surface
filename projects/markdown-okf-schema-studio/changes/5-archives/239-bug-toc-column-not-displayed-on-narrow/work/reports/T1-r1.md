## Change
Narrow pane (< 760 px) with the TOC toggle on: a 16 px rail on the right with one tick per heading
(section at the reading line highlighted, `aria-current`). Hover or focus on the rail opens the TOC
nav as an overlay (`data-sphinx-preview-toc-overlay="open"`, CSS-only display). It closes 300 ms
after pointer and focus have left both rail and overlay, at once on Escape (window keydown, so a
hover-only open closes too; focus returns to the rail only if it was inside), on an entry click, or
when the TOC is turned off. A tick click goes to its heading. Rail: `role=group`, `tabindex=0`,
label "Table of contents rail"; the overlay is the existing `nav` (aria-label "Table of contents").
The overlay has no animation, so `prefers-reduced-motion` needs no special case (the existing
reduced-motion tests forbid a non-`!important` animation block). The rail is built the first time
the TOC is open (keeps the T33 startup trace unchanged). Wide panes are unchanged.
