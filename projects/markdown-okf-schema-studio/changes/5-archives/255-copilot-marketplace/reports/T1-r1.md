## Catalog checked against the official reference

I fetched https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/reference/copilot-cli-reference/cli-plugin-reference.

Top level:
- Required: `name` (kebab-case, at most 64 characters), `owner` (`{name, email?}`), `plugins`.
- Optional: `metadata` (`{description?, version?, pluginRoot?}`).

Plugin entry:
- Required: `name`, `source`.
- Optional: `description` (at most 1024 characters), `version`, `author` (`{name, email?, url?}`), `homepage`, `repository`, `license`, `keywords`, `category`.

Every key in the catalog appears in these lists. The Claude catalog's `owner.url` is not part of the Copilot owner object, so I left it out. The reference's lookup order puts `.github/plugin/marketplace.json` before `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`.
