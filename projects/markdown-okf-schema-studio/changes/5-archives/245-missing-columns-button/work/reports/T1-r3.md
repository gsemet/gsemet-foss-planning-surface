## Design
The Grid form gets three more fields: Headers, Footers, Links. Each holds one entry per card, separated by `|`.
Entry N becomes `:header:`, `:footer:` or `:link:` of card N (same `cardText` as the Card form); empty or missing entries
leave the option out. More entries than cards is invalid: the form stays open and says why. A `|` inside a link is read as a separator.
