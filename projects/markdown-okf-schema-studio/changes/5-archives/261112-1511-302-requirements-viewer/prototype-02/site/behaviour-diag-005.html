Behaviour DIAG-005

Mermaid colours follow the preview theme

When the page draws a Mermaid diagram, the extension SHALL take its colours from the preview theme variables, use a built-in fallback for any variable that is missing, empty or a CSS system colour keyword, and draw it again when the theme colours change.

Step 1 of path 2 in DIAG-001: from “The diagram is drawn as a picture” to “The diagram is redrawn in the new theme, labels kept”.

Known limits (5)

- Known issue: The visual check covers dark, white and cream and only node fills; border and secondary colours have no check.
- Known issue: The redraw evidence covers dark, white and cream; the light mode relies on the same observer.
- Known issue: The fallback colours are fixed colours of a light palette.
- Known issue: No unit test covers the fallback logic.
- Known issue: Edge label readability is checked by the visual campaign only; no unit test.
