# T1 round 1 report (CR 130)

## Change
Whichever moved last owns the block actions. `html[data-sphinx-preview-actions-owner]` is
`keyboard` when a block selection renders, `pointer` after a real mouse move (same-coordinate
synthetic moves after a scroll are ignored), unset when the selection is cleared. The bar under
the selection's last block carries `data-sphinx-actions-shown`; CSS shows it under `keyboard`,
hides the hovered block's bar under `keyboard`, and removes the selection outline under `pointer`.
Up/down already act on the block of the bar they sit on (unchanged).
