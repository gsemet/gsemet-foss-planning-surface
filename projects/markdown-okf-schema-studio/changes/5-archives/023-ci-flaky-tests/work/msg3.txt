fix(render): keep a leading properties table clear of the toolbar

On a slow load the Description toggle of a leading properties table could
stay under the floating toolbar: the table's own size changes and a late
page or font load were not watched, and updates skipped during a busy
second were never replayed. The table now re-measures when it resizes,
when the page and fonts finish loading, and once after a skipped burst.
