fix(editor): keep fences whole and refuse moves that spoil a box

In an admonition or an alert, opening or erasing a block held by a list item no
longer takes a line of a directive nested beside it. The move chord now refuses
to put a `---` line first in a box, or a heading first in an admonition without
a title or in a callout, with a reason, instead of hiding the content or turning
the heading into the title.
