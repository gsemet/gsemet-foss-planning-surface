## Open points

1. The owner decision says the list shows above the caret. For a head line in the block editor this cannot happen (the head is always the first line under the bar). Needs a product decision.
2. With the list open, a further ArrowLeft or ArrowRight inside the type word closes the list and keeps it closed, so the word cannot be stepped through with the list open. Probably unintended; not changed.
3. The MyST list is cut at its bottom edge; check its max-height.
4. The scenarios assert "open, at the caret, inside the viewport", not "above".
