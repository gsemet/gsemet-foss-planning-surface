## Headline result

Every option (context 180k to 250k, wakes 30 to 100, yes delay 0 to 8 h, with or without the Drivers
restart) loses money or never fires; a fresh session is ready at 150k to 195k tokens and $0.7 to
$1.9, while a compaction (at about 267k tokens, not the 162k to 170k the host card measured) lands at
85k to 114k. Recommendation: no threshold hand-over; if wanted for quality, opt-in extension of
"Handing the seat over".
