## Choices to know

- Top rows' `batch` is the record's batch string (`"batch-29"`); the point's `n` is the number.
- Headlines with no covered record are `None` (also `per_cr.cost`/`merged`, `spend.total`,
  `calls.total`, `crs.held`/`merged`).
- A part or model key outside the known set is kept (parts after `agents`, sorted), never dropped.
- An `outside` dict with no present part is no figure (not 0); a present part that is not a dict
  or lacks a number `usd` voids the record's outside.
- The `unsplit` rest is added when segments sum to cost minus at least 0.005; batch-29 gives none.
- Segments are kept even at usd 0 (the spec's literal rule); no record has one today.
