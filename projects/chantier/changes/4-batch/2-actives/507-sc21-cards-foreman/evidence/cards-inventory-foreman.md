# SC21 evidence: inventory of the Foreman's rules

Round 2, sentence level. Ids (`FM-nnn`, `FM-Bnnn`) are drafts that child 26 replaces. The new card and topics are in `skills/chantier/cards-next/` (`foreman.md`, `topics/`, `topics.json`).

## How the sources were walked

- Sources: `skills/chantier/references/foreman.md` (393 lines) and its eight pieces at the worktree's base: foreman-stack (197 lines), foreman-merge (139), foreman-hold (87), foreman-lost-driver (130), foreman-conflict (45), foreman-release (48), foreman-refused (163), foreman-pause (25). Headings and blank lines carry no rule and get no row.
- One row per rule sentence: a sentence ends at a full stop outside a code span; a sentence of more than 190 characters is cut again at a top-level semicolon. A row's `source` is the line, or the two lines, where its sentence sits; no row spans a block. `behaviour` is the sentence itself, cut at 240 characters. The walk was run by a script (`work/coder/split.py`, `gen.py`, `out.py`, `doc.py`, in the CR folder) and every row was then read through the three review lists below.
- The B rows (`FM-B###`) are the sentences of "The user's boundaries" (`foreman.md:59-184`, specification section 4); their `trigger` starts with the specification's label (B1a to B7f).
- Round 7: FM-B003, FM-B009 and FM-B037 are now kind a (their label protects and the sentence carries it); FM-B020 is now kind e (B1k does not protect). FM-B063 to FM-B065 are sentences the earlier rounds left inside FM-B002 and FM-B055. The label to final home table is `boundaries-split.md`.
- Kinds (study-fable section 3): a protects the user or the repository; b inferable by a capable model; c incident or retro patch; d host trial and "unproved" bookkeeping; e state or procedure the CLI prints just in time, or a topic carries; f duplicate. A kind a row has a home in the card, the floor or a CLI refusal, never a topic alone (F5), and no protecting row is inferable. A procedure carried by a topic only is kind e (or c), not a.
- New home vocabulary: `card foreman.md:<n>`, `floor <n>` (`floor.md`), `refusal <command, message start>`, `next:` line (the line the CLI prints), `topic <name>`, `host row`, `inferable`, `duplicate`.
- Checks: `grep -c '<phrase>' foreman.md` names a phrase to find in the card; `help <topic>` means `$CHANTIER help <topic>` prints it (`load_topics` loads `topics.json`); `none (<why>)` where a refusal or the floor owns the rule, or it is dropped.
- Roles: every topic row carries `foreman` and `chief-of-staff` (the Chief of Staff steers a direct batch with this card). The role column of a row is `foreman`: the card's reader.
