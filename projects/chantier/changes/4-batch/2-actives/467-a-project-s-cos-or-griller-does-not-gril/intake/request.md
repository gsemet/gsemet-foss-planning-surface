# A project's CoS or Griller does not grill another project's CR

Filed on the owner's words of 2026-11-23, relayed by the Chief of Staff of okf-schema. To grill later with the owner, not now.

The owner's words (French): "rajoute une CR pour que le griller et CoS d'un projet ne veuillent pas par defaut griller une CR autre que son projet."

What happened: in a planning surface with several projects, the Chief of Staff of okf-schema filed two CRs in the project chantier's inbox (a CR the owner asked it to relay), then offered to grill them. The CRs were not in its project; their grill belongs to chantier's own Chief of Staff or a Griller of chantier.

In short: a Chief of Staff or a Griller of one project must not, by default, grill, specify, order or run a CR of another project of the surface. It may file (relay) an item in another project's inbox on the owner's word, and says the CR waits for that project's own Chief of Staff or Griller. It offers no grill, spec, batch or order for it. Only the owner's explicit word for that CR (for example "grill it here") lifts the default.

Points to settle at the grill: where the rule lives (the Chief of Staff card, the Griller card, or both, and the session-start card's seat and role part); whether the CLI refuses an `order`, `open` or grill-side write on a CR whose project differs from the session's `--project`, or the cards only say it; what `file` into another project prints as the next step.
