# Original visualisations on other board pages, in the spirit of the Costs receipt

The user (2026-11-17): "I love the ticket view [the Costs page's receipt]: it's simple, visual,
fun. Could you propose another original kind of visualisation on another page?" Four ideas were
proposed (a time card per CR, a station departure board, a building under construction, a telex
tape for the Journal); the user asked for a prototype of each. The prototypes are in
`intake/prototypes.html` (batch-24's real CRs, states, rounds, costs and PRs; times are made up).
