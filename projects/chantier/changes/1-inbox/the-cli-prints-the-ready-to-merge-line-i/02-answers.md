Short name: `ready-line`

1. ready-line-Q1: **Where the ready line is printed.** A. In `status`, one line per CR (ready to merge, or in draft with "do not merge" and what it waits for), the label's icon, the full PR link, each gap, "do not merge before X" when a step is owed by the Chief of Staff, the local gate line.
2. ready-line-Q2: **The batch state at each call.** A. One complete `batch` line: shape, slots, steering, rounds limit, budget, who owes the local gate, who merges.
3. ready-line-Q3: **Rules after a compaction.** A. The reload of the Chief of Staff and the Foreman reads a short piece of rules (the report layout, the ready words, the shape rule, who merges).
4. ready-line-Q4: **Grilled enough.** A. Specify, for the running batch batch-31.

Destination: running batch batch-31.

5. ready-line-Q5: **When the clause appears.** A. For every open step (local gate not passed at its head, an open order on the CR), naming who owes it.
6. ready-line-Q6: **Batch line and the gate rule.** A. It lists the ready CRs whose local gate is open; the per-shape rule stays in the cards.
7. ready-line-Q7: **Reload over the byte limit.** A. A rules pointer on status's batch line (no counted bytes) instead of handing back blocked.

8. ready-line-Q8: **The local gate clause.** A. Show what the CLI records: the clause is named long gate; the Chief of Staff adds the owed local run from the Foreman's ask, which wins when the two differ.
9. ready-line-Q9: **Who merges under a grant.** A. `merges: the user` plus, when the record holds a grant, `under the grant: the release/fix PR (steering session)`; reload-rules.md says the same.
10. ready-line-Q10: **The line's prefix.** A. `handed back:` (never said to the user).
11. ready-line-Q11: **A step owed on a green CR.** A. `owed before the merge: <X> (<who>)`; the user may still merge, recorded as any merge.
