# A project's CoS or Griller does not grill another project's CR

Short name: `own-project-grill`. Owner's words of 2026-11-23, relayed from okf-schema.

By default a Chief of Staff or a Griller of one project does not grill, specify, order or run a CR of another project of the surface. It may file (relay) an item into another project's inbox on the user's word, and says the CR waits for that project's own Chief of Staff or Griller; it offers no grill, specification, batch or order for it. Only the user's explicit word for that CR lifts the default. The rule is in the Chief of Staff card, the Griller card and `session-start.md`'s seat and role part; the CLI adds no refusal (it already refuses a CR folder outside its project's planning folder).
