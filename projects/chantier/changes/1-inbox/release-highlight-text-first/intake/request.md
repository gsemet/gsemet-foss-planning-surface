# Release highlights: text before the picture, cropped to the change

Priority P2. From the user, relayed by the Studio project's Chief of Staff, 2026-11-20, a follow-up of CR-413. The user's words: "j aimerais que le paragraphe commence par le texte, et que l'image soit cropper sur le point d attention".

Target: `skills/chantier/references/release-template.md`, "A highlight" (and "Visible evidence" for the crop).

1. **Text first.** A highlight is its heading, then at most two sentences, then the picture. Today the template has heading, picture, text.
2. **Crop on the point of attention.** Each picture shows only the part of the screen where the change happens: no toolbar, empty margin or unrelated content. In a joined before|after image, both halves are cropped to the same region, at the same scale.

Kept: "When the picture shows the whole change, write no text" (then the highlight is its heading and the picture). Studio carries the same change in its own guideline until chantier's template has it. Pairs with release-process-notes-align (docs/release-process.md), which can carry it in the same batch.
