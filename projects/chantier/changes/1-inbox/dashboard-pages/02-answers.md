4. dashboard-Q4 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **Tabs.** Two tabs, Productivity and Spending; Productivity opens first. No "Scorers" tab: chantier records no score. Reason: only what the records hold.

6. dashboard-Q6 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **The time axis: one point per closed batch, not per day.** Every chart has one point per closed batch, oldest to newest, labelled with the batch's number, its closing day shown with its value. Reason: the records hold costs and results per batch and per CR, never per day; a batch often runs over two days (batch-29: 19 to 21 November), and a per-day chart would put its whole cost on one day and show a false zero on the other. A true per-day chart needs dated costs, which is new data: out of scope, a CR of its own. The per-day reading of what was done is the History page's.
7. dashboard-Q7 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **The range.** A selector with three choices: the last 10 closed batches (default), the last 20, all. No date picker, no day/week/month selector, no previous/next arrows (those are the History page's). The headline numbers follow the range. Reason: the least control that makes 30 and more batches readable.
8. dashboard-Q8 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **Productivity: what it shows.** Four tiles. (a) CRs: per batch, the CRs the batch held and the CRs merged, two lines, with the range's totals as the headline (in place of the example's "pull requests opened vs merged": a CR has one PR). (b) Rounds per merged CR: the batch's mean, one line, with the range's mean as the headline (chantier's own measure of rework, in place of "human interactions per PR"). (c) Batch duration: opening to closing, one line, with the range's median as the headline, for the batches whose record holds both dates (in place of the example's cycle times). (d) Model calls by part: steering, Drivers, workers, stacked, with the range's total as the headline, for the batches whose record holds the split (in place of "runs by agent type"; the title says "model calls", not "runs"). Reason: each is the nearest figure the records already hold to one tile of the example.
9. dashboard-Q9 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **Spending: what it shows.** Three tiles. (a) Cost per merged CR: the batch's cost divided by its merged CRs, one line, with the range's figure as the headline (total cost, merged CRs and their ratio). (b) Spend per batch, stacked, with a switch "by part" (steering, Drivers, workers) or "by model", the same switch as the Costs page's card; a batch whose record holds a cost but no split is drawn as one neutral segment named "not split"; the headline is the range's total, and one line under it gives the cost recorded outside the batches (grill, Chief of Staff's own steering) for the range, where the records hold it, not added to the total. (c) The most expensive CRs of the range: the top 10 by recorded cost, one line each (CR, title on one line, batch, rounds, cost), the CR's PR opened by a click in a new tab. Reason: the example's three blocks, with chantier's own sources (part and model) in place of "source".
10. dashboard-Q10 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **A figure the record lacks.** Never a zero and never a guess: a batch with no recorded cost, date or split has no point in the charts that need it, the line is cut there, and each tile says under its headline how many batches of the range it covers ("7 of 10 batches"). Reason: the records are uneven (dates and costs from batch-17, the split from batch-23, the models from batch-24), and a false zero would mislead.
11. dashboard-Q11 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **The running batch and odd batches.** Closed batches only: the running or paused batch has no point, and one line of the page says so with a link to the Costs page, which holds its live figure. A record that is not closed (an abandoned batch) is left out. A dropped or cancelled CR counts in "CRs held" and its cost stays in its batch's cost, but it is not a merged CR. Reason: a live, moving point among final ones would surprise, and Costs already owns the live figure.
12. dashboard-Q12 (2026-11-21, Fable advisor): **Reading a value.** Every tile has a title, a headline number and a legend with words; a point's exact value (batch, closing day, figure) shows on hover, on keyboard focus and on a tap. Reason: readable by mouse, keyboard and phone, with the least interaction.
