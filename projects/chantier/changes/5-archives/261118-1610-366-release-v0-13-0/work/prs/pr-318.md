# PR 318 feat: switch a Foreman batch to direct mode with CRs in flight
## Summary

A Foreman batch could not be taken over by the Chief of Staff: when its Foreman stopped (for example at the usage limit) with several CRs in flight, the only ways out were to drop the work or start a new Foreman. This PR lets the user say "switch the batch to direct mode": the Chief of Staff claims the next Foreman number itself, the batch becomes direct, and every CR whose Driver was stopped restarts through the existing restart path. Queued CRs stay queued; PRs, branches and worktrees are kept; the old Foreman's spend stays in the batch's cost. The switch to Foreman mode is unchanged and can be undone and redone.

## Deliverables

The switch is the Chief of Staff's own claim, with no new command:

```sh
$CHANTIER foreman set <session id> --epoch <n+1>
$CHANTIER foreman transcript <transcript id> --epoch <n+1>
# -> transcript <uuid>
# -> steering direct: the Chief of Staff steers <batch> as Foreman n°<n+1>
```

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foreman batch, claim by the recorded Chief of Staff transcript | stays a Foreman batch | becomes direct, a `steering` event is logged, the old number is stale (exit 3) |
| Same transcript claims again after another one | no new claim recorded | a new claim is recorded: priced per window, current for `close` |
| `close` with a replaced Foreman whose transcript ends on a tool call | blocked by it | listed apart, never a cause |

Choices: the CLI recognises the Chief of Staff by the batch's recorded Chief of Staff transcript (the key the opposite flip already uses), so no new refusal and no flag; a Foreman not answering is stopped from outside (by the user, or on Claude Desktop by the Chief of Staff on the user's yes), and each old Driver is checked ended with `close` before its CR restarts.

## Blast radius

Grade: **2**. The claim path and the cost and close readers are shared code; a batch with one claim per transcript behaves as before. Rolling back is a revert; the cards and docs are text.

## Changes

- `commands/seat.py`: `foreman transcript` flips a Foreman batch to direct when the claimed transcript is the recorded Chief of Staff transcript, logs a `steering` event, prints a steering line; a claim by a transcript differing from the last claim is recorded again.
- `rails.py`, `costs/transcripts.py`, `costs/sessions.py`, `commands/cost/pricing.py`, `commands/cost/sessions.py`: the current steering session is the last claim; each transcript is priced over each of its windows.
- Cards: `chief-of-staff.md` (new "Switching to direct mode", entry row, recovery and offering options), `foreman.md` (the switch rule and the stops), `concepts.md`, `routing.md`, `driver.md`, `session-start.md`, `hosts/claude-code.md` (stop another session row); docs `use-cases-direct.md`, `use-cases-foreman.md`.
- Tests and the scenario's direct unit extended.

## Human decisions

- No new command or flag: the Chief of Staff's own claim of the next Foreman number makes the batch direct.
- A Foreman counts as not answering when its heartbeat is late and one ping gets no reply within 5 minutes, or on the user's word; a live Foreman stops its own Drivers first.
- Before restarting a CR, the old Driver must be shown ended by `close`'s check; stopping a session is never assumed to end its children.
- The switch can be undone by the existing switch to Foreman mode, and the round-trip cost and `close` fix stays in this change.
- The board's cost receipt after a switch is left to a separate change.

## Impacts

No visual change. `status`, `STATUS.md`, `cost` and the board say "direct" after the switch.
