# Answers

- Q: Does the rule cover the rebase note only, or every note that carries work to a Driver? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): Every note that carries work (a `rebase:`, an order note, an answer), with the rebase note named as the first case. The race is the same for all of them, and one sentence covers them at no extra cost.
- Q: Which read: a plain `status`, or the wake's `status --refresh --epoch`? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): A plain `$CHANTIER status`, which moves nothing. The read is a check before an action, not a wake, and must not count as a heartbeat.
- Q: Does the rule hold in direct mode too? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): Yes, for both steerings: the incident happened in a direct run, and the Chief of Staff follows the Foreman's card for its Drivers. The rule is written once, in the Foreman's card, and direct mode must visibly inherit it.
- Q: For a CR found `ready`, is the whole take-back path run, or a shorter one for a rebase? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): The whole path as the card has it (`agents set <cr> -`, `take <cr> --note`, the Driver resumed with `note taken back:`, `agents set` again). A second, shorter path would be a new thing to get wrong; the fix is to choose the existing path at the right moment.
- Q: Does the Driver's side change, so that a `rebase:` note received just after a hand-back is acted on at once? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): No. The CR is `ready` in the record and its `handback` would be refused; the Driver passing the note up stays the net for the hand-back that lands between the `status` and the message.
- Q: Should the CLI close the race (a command that sends the note, or refuses it on a ready CR)? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): No. Messaging is the host's, the CLI knows no role, and one lost exchange in a rare window does not measure up to a new tool.
- Q: Is the remaining window (a hand-back between the read and the message) accepted? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): Yes. It is seconds wide, costs one exchange when it happens, and loses no work.
