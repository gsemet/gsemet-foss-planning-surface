Answer 10 (a CR still blocked at the report), from the CLI:
  `pause` refuses while any CR is live, queued, `ready` or `blocked` (commands.pause), and `close`
  refuses an unpaused batch (commands._close_target). So a blocked CR blocks both. The only way
  out is `order drop <cr>` (commands.order: trash_cr, status cancelled; folder to 6-trash/). The
  runbook step: on the user's word, `order drop`, then the Chief of Staff moves the folder back
  to 1-inbox/ by hand; the reason goes in the report.
