- Q: What does the steering session do with an Auto-fix event on a CR whose Driver is live? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): nothing on the PR: no fix, no merge, no rebase, and no relay to the Driver; it ends the turn at once. The Driver reads its own CI and already holds the branch, and a relay would spend messages against the host's limit for news the Driver has.
- Q: And when the CR has no live Driver (handed back ready, waiting for the user)? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): the event is only a signal: the steering session checks the forge itself and, if the PR is really red or in conflict, follows the card's usual route (the CR goes back to a Driver with a note); it never does what the event asks itself. The existing rules already cover a ready PR that turns red, and the Foreman never codes or pushes.
- Q: Does the rule hold in direct mode, where the Chief of Staff steers? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): yes, the same rule for whichever session steers. PR 137 was a direct run, and in both modes the fix is a Driver's.

- Q: Does chantier turn Auto-fix off, or unbind the PRs, by itself? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): no, never; no card uses the app's PR monitor, to arm it or to disarm it.
