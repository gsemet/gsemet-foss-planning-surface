# Answers

Given by the user on 2026-11-06, in the Chief of Staff's session.

- **What to prove:** ideally the target project says; the CR Driver makes sure that before and
  after evidence can be captured and is planned, according to the kind of change, and can rely on
  the project's e2e test environments and use the same technique.
- **Plan:** the Investigator proposes it before the first round; the CR Driver validates it and
  stays responsible.
- **Capture:** the Verifier, before and after, at the end, with the same technique.
- **Evidence that cannot be captured:** the Verifier writes why and the CR goes on, unless the
  project's rules require it; then its absence is NOT VERIFIED.
- **Form:** a CR of its own, apart from `stack-demo`.
- **Where the rule lives:** in the project's harness, its `AGENTS.md`, as its ordinary text; no
  `evidence:` line.
- **Media:** forges take attachments, so the evidence goes in the PR or MR; the wording stays
  generic for GitHub and GitLab.
