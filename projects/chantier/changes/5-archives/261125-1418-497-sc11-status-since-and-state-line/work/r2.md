   the wrappers keep every file under 400.

   `status/advice.py` imports it.

   lease renewal is not the round's work.

   filled in for `<n>`.
5. `next <cr> --lease <n>` on a CR with no lease recorded is accepted (no claim to lose).
6. `restack_line` moved from `orders_merged.py` to `status/due.py` so that the due list and
   `order merged` print the same words.

   declares no `--epoch` flag, so the spec's `--epoch <N>` there (open question 1's wording)
   would advise a refused command.
8. The `--lease` help text of `next`: "the lease number `take` printed: checked, never renewed; a
   stale one exits 3 (`claim lost`)".
9. Scenario: the hand-back's `next:` line is checked against its fixed words ("send the hand-back
   line above to the Foreman"), not against `next <cr> --lease <n>`, which the ended lease
   refuses after a hand-back. The in-round step and `phase <cr> verifier` print no `next:` line
   of their own: the Driver reads `next <cr> --lease <n>` after them. The stale lease is made
   with `lease <cr> hold --minutes 1` under `CHANTIER_LEASE_TEST_MINUTE=3` (as `lease_cases`
   does), not with `renew` under the short TTL: a renewal never shortens a live expiry. The case
   calls `w.sync_gates()` first, since an earlier case of the unit removed `chantier.yaml`.
