Round 3. Ids (`D-nnn`) are drafts that child 26 replaces. The new cards and topics are in `skills/chantier/cards-next/`.

## How the sources were walked

- The sources are `skills/chantier/references/driver.md` (658 lines) and its 16 pieces, at the worktree's base: driver-session (42 lines), driver-refused (61), driver-retake (109), driver-matrix (27), driver-trouble (173), driver-conflicts (89), driver-handback-other (68), driver-pr-extras (76), stack-driver-changes (129), stack-driver-joins (26), stack-driver-make (62), stack-driver-restack (41), release-driver-end (18), release-driver-merged (54), release-driver-notes (38), release-driver-ship (14).
- Same columns and kinds as `cards-inventory-workers.md`: kind a protects the user or the repository, b inferable, c incident, d trial bookkeeping, e CLI-printable, f duplicate. When unsure, a. An F5 rule (a rule that protects) is never "inferable": it sits in a card (Never), the floor or a refusal.
- One row per rule or bullet of `driver.md`, by its line range; a long bullet that holds several rules is split in the `behaviour` column and shares one row. Pieces: one row per section or bullet group, with the piece's file and line range. Pure transitions ("Next:", "read X when Y") get no row.
- "topic (new)" marks a rule that `chantier next` does not print, so that no `next:` line can carry it: it has its home in the new topics `cr-workers`, `cr-prompt`, `cr-rounds`, `cr-ship`, `cr-proof` or `cr-handback`.
- The old moment/startup machinery (`startup-manifest.json`, "Startup core", "Another Driver's section are not opening reads") is replaced by `brief` and `help`: those rows are kind e and say so.
- Checks: `grep` names a phrase to look for in the new card or topic; `help` means `$CHANTIER help <topic>` prints it (load_topics ok, sc20-links.md); `quiz` names a case id under `evals/quiz/<role>/` (real run not done (child 28), so these are unproved, sc20-quiz.md).
