# Answers

- Q: Who records the pass: the Driver, or the Inspector itself (as the Verifier records its own verdict)? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): The Driver, with the verdict and the `head` copied from the Inspector's JSON. The Inspector stays read-only with its reply as its only output; a second writer in the record is a larger change than the defect asks for.
- Q: A note, or a new record in the CLI like `verdict`? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): A `note`. It is already in the CR's record and its log, no hand-back depends on the Inspector's pass, and a new CLI record would come from no measured cost.
- Q: Which passes are recorded? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): Every `APPROVE`: a round's, a body-only re-inspection, the inspection of a move (a rebase or a restack that changed the patch), and each review step of the `Team:`; a `FIX-REQUIRED` needs none, since the next round's brief carries it. The run's three failures came from passes the round's commit note does not cover.
- Q: Which SHA does the PR body's Review box cite? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): The one the note holds for the reviewed state at the PR's head: the commit of the approved round (the note has the reviewed `head` and `committed as <sha>`), or the head of the move for a later pass. The Inspector's `head` predates the round's commit, so the note must tie the two for the box to be checkable.
- Q: What does the Verifier accept as proof of the Review box? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): The Driver's note of the pass in the CR's record, with the SHA the box cites; with none, or another SHA, `NOT VERIFIED` as today. It is proof that a review happened and where, not of the code, which the Verifier still proves itself.
- Q: In what order: the note, then the body? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): The note first, always; the Review box is filled or updated only from a recorded note, and a box whose pass is not recorded stays unchecked. That order is the whole fix.
- Q: Does the rule bind the stack driver too? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): Yes: when it starts an `inspector` after a move and updates a PR's Test plan SHAs, it records the pass on that CR first. It edits the same box and would fail the same way.
- Q: Should `handback ready` be refused when no Inspector pass is recorded? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): No. The CLI knows no role and does not read notes; the Verifier's judgment of the description is the check.
