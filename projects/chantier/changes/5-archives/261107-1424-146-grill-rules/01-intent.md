# Intent: answers recorded at once, and a shared understanding before "grilled enough?"

The Chief of Staff's card (`skills/chantier/references/chief-of-staff.md`, "Backlog and grill")
says to record every answer in `02-answers.md`, but not when. In the grill of 2026-11-07 the
Chief of Staff held four rounds of answers in its context and wrote none to disk until the user
asked; a compaction would have lost them.

The user wants two rules, stated in the card:

1. **Record at once.** Every human answer and decision about a CR is written into that CR's
   folder (`02-answers.md`) immediately after each answer, before the next question or any
   other work. That covers a decision given outside a grill (priority, "left aside", "same
   batch").
2. **Shared understanding first.** The Chief of Staff may ask whether a CR is grilled enough
   only after a complete restatement of the CR in the user's language (what it changes, every
   decision taken, what is still open) that the user can correct. It never decides on its own
   that a CR is grilled enough (rule already given: the user decides, the Chief of Staff asks).

To settle in the spec: whether the restatement is also written to the folder (e.g. a section of
`02-answers.md`); whether `just scenario` or a structure test should check anything (likely not:
these are judgement rules of a card).
