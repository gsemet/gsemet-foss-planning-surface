# Intent

Once a stack is ready, the user has PRs to read and little sense of what they deliver as a whole.
They want the Chief of Staff, when idle, to print its cost report and offer a guided demo of the
stack: what is delivered, the features touched, the bugs fixed, the important architectural impact,
with the evidence found in the PRs (before and after screenshots when there are some).

It then guides the user to try it themselves: where to click, which commands, a fixture or a small
ephemeral environment if needed, and for each change what was there before and what they should
see now. It is close to a QA driver's session, except that the user does the testing and the scope
is what the stack delivers.
