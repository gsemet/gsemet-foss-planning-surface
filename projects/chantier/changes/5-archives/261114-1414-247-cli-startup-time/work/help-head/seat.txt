usage: chantier seat [-h] [--env ENV] [--title TITLE] [--link LINK]
                     [--over SESSION]
                     [{get,take,leave}] [session]

positional arguments:
  {get,take,leave}
  session           the session id, for take and leave

options:
  -h, --help        show this help message and exit
  --env ENV         take: the machine's name, or Remote
  --title TITLE     take: the session's current title
  --link LINK       take: the session's link
  --over SESSION    take: the session whose seat this one takes, on the user's
                    word

exit=0
