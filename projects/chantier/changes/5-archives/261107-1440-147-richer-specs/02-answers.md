# Answers (grill of 2026-11-07)

- **A spec-writer agent** writes the Change Specification, not the Chief of Staff.
- **Only on the user's word:** the user decides when a CR is grilled enough. The Chief of Staff
  never decides it; it asks.
- **In the background:** the Chief of Staff starts it as a background agent. The CR then shows a
  state "specifying"; once the spec is written it is "specified".
- **A specified CR can go to a CR driver**, but it may stay in the inbox or the backlog: the user
  alone decides the priority of implementation.
- **Model: Opus 5.5.**
- **Always possible, even while a batch runs:** it is outside any batch, takes no slot and no
  budget of it; its cost goes on the Chief of Staff's line.
- To settle in the spec: the agent files on both hosts; how the state is recorded for an inbox or
  backlog item; the Chief of Staff's review of the spec before showing it.
