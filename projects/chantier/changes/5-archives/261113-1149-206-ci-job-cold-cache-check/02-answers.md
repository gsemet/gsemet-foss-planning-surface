# Answers

- Q: Which changes owe the cold-cache run: eval-kit changes that add a CI job only, or any change to this repository that adds one? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): any change that adds a CI job, or changes the steps of one. The fault is about a job's first run on a runner, not about the eval kit, and such changes are rare, so the wider rule costs almost nothing.
- Q: Does an ordinary change (no CI job touched) owe it too? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): no. A cold run downloads everything again; paid on every change it would cost more than the one round it saves.
- Q: What exactly is run with the cold cache? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): the job's own steps, in the workflow's order, in one fresh empty `UV_CACHE_DIR` (so a warm-up step such as the one before `just eval-dry-run` is run too, and proved). Running the recipe alone would fail by design where the job has a warm-up step, and would not prove the job.
- Q: Is the cold run's output kept as evidence? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): yes, it is the Verifier's evidence and the PR body quotes it, as "Changing the kit" already asks for the dry run. A rule with no trace is not checked by the Inspector.
- Q: Where does the cold-cache rule live: in a card or in this project's own rules? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): in this project's rules (`CONTRIBUTING.md`, daily work, and `evals/README.md`, "Changing the kit"), not in a card. `uv` and its cache are this repository's tools; a card serves every project and must not name them.
- Q: What does the Spec-writer card gain? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): one generic line: a specification that adds or changes a CI job names the environment the job runs in (the system, and what is absent there: caches, tools, credentials) and the evidence that proves it before the push. It applies to any project and names no tool; the project's rules say how.
- Q: Does the Spec-writer decide the environment when the answers do not give it? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): it reads it from the project's CI files, read-only, as it reads the code; what it cannot find goes in its list of open questions. The card already forbids it to invent a decision.
- Q: Does the rule cover differences other than the cache (system, installed tools)? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): no, the local proof is the cold cache only; the specification names the other differences so the Driver knows CI is their only proof. Emulating a runner is a tool no measurement asks for.
- Q: Does this CR add a recipe (`just ci-cold …`) or a structure test? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): no, text only. AGENTS.md wants new tools from measured cost, and one case is not a measure.
- Q: Which gates does this CR pass? — Advisor (Fable, answering in the user's place on the user's word of 2026-11-12): `just check` and, for the card's line, `just scenario`. AGENTS.md says so for a change to the cards; the docs part alone would need none.
